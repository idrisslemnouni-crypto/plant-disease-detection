# Real field-photo source

Original author: Makerere AI Lab with NaCRRI Uganda. Homepage https://github.com/AI-Lab-Makerere/ibean ; author-hosted mirror https://huggingface.co/datasets/AI-Lab-Makerere/beans . Source licence MIT retained in SOURCE-LICENSE.txt. Archive URLs, exact mirror commit, SHA-256 and actual image counts are pinned in source-manifest.json. GCS links from the original README returned 403 during acquisition; do not silently switch mirrors or metadata revisions.

Images have three labels: angular_leaf_spot, bean_rust, healthy. Preserve supplied train/validation/test partitions. Exact decoded-pixel duplicates are excluded with test > validation > train precedence. Perceptual dHash screening is diagnostic; no unknown plant identity or farm grouping is invented. Raw archives and images remain ignored in data/raw. All displayed photos are attributed to this source.
